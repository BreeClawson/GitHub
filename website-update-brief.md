## 3. Restructure Services as a Linear Pipeline (not a flat grid)

Replace the current services grid/pill layout with a **sequential, connected flow** — numbered steps, arrows, or a timeline component. Do not display these as equal, unconnected cards.

**Pipeline order:**

1. **Audit** — Full digital infrastructure assessment, gap analysis, findings report with prioritized recommendations.
   *Copy: "Before anything else, we find out what's actually going on. No guessing, no assumptions — just a clear picture of what's working and what's not."*

2. **Systems Build** *(conditional step — only shown/pitched if the audit finds a gap)*
   *Copy: "If the audit uncovers missing infrastructure — POS, inventory, backend systems — we build it before touching anything customer-facing."*

3. **Strategy** — Built once the foundation is confirmed solid (either it was already in place, or it was just built in step 2).
   *Copy: "Once the systems are solid, we build the actual plan: what to say, where to say it, who it's for."*

4. **Social Media Marketing & Management (SMM)** — Ongoing execution of the strategy.
   *Copy: "This is usually where people think the work starts. It's actually step four — by the time we get here, the guesswork is already gone."*

5. **Add-Ons** — Newsletter, ebook, logo design, etc., surfacing organically once inside the relationship.
   *Copy: "Once the system is running, new opportunities show up — a newsletter, an ebook, a new offer. We build those as they come up."*

**Key instruction for Claude Code:** "Social media marketing and management always come after systems are confirmed solid — either because the audit found them already in place, or because they were just built. SMM is never step one."
